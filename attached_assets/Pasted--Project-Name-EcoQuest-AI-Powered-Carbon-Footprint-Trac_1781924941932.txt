# Project Name

EcoQuest – AI-Powered Carbon Footprint Tracker and Sustainability Companion

# Objective

Build a modern, responsive, production-ready full-stack web application that helps individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through simple actions, personalized insights, and gamification.

The application should educate users about their environmental impact, motivate sustainable behavior, and make carbon reduction engaging and rewarding.

# Target Users

* Students
* Working professionals
* Families
* Environmentally conscious individuals
* Anyone who wants to reduce their carbon footprint

# Design Requirements

* Modern, clean UI inspired by Apple and Google Material Design.
* Green, white, and light blue color palette.
* Mobile-first responsive design.
* Dark mode support.
* Smooth animations and transitions.
* Accessible and easy to navigate.
* Professional hackathon-quality appearance.

# Tech Stack

Frontend:

* React
* Tailwind CSS

Backend:

* Firebase

Services:

* Firebase Authentication
* Firestore Database
* Firebase Hosting

Charts:

* Chart.js or Recharts

# Pages and Features

## 1. Landing Page

Sections:

* Hero section with the slogan:
  "Track your footprint. Change your future."
* Short explanation of carbon footprints.
* Features section.
* Statistics section.
* Call-to-action buttons:

  * Get Started
  * Calculate My Footprint
* Testimonials section with placeholder data.
* Footer with contact and social links.

## 2. Authentication

Features:

* Sign Up
* Login
* Forgot Password
* Google Sign-In
* Persistent sessions.

## 3. User Onboarding Questionnaire

Collect:

* Country
* Age group
* Transportation habits:

  * Walking
  * Bicycle
  * Car
  * Motorcycle
  * Public transport
* Daily travel distance.
* Monthly electricity bill.
* Household size.
* Food preferences:

  * Vegetarian
  * Non-vegetarian
  * Vegan
* Online shopping frequency.
* Air travel frequency.
* Waste and recycling habits.

Store all responses in the database.

## 4. Carbon Footprint Calculator

Calculate:

* Transportation emissions.
* Electricity emissions.
* Food emissions.
* Shopping emissions.
* Lifestyle emissions.

Display:

* Daily emissions.
* Monthly emissions.
* Annual emissions.

Provide visual indicators:

* Low Impact
* Moderate Impact
* High Impact

Show comparisons:

* Equivalent trees needed.
* Equivalent car kilometers.
* Equivalent household energy usage.

## 5. Dashboard

Show:

* Carbon score.
* Progress charts.
* Emissions breakdown.
* Current eco streak.
* Total green points.
* Earned badges.
* Daily challenges.
* Personalized recommendations.

Charts:

* Weekly emissions.
* Monthly emissions.
* Category distribution.
* Carbon reduction trend.

## 6. AI-Powered Recommendations

Generate personalized suggestions such as:

* Use public transport twice a week.
* Reduce meat consumption.
* Switch to LED lighting.
* Unplug devices when not in use.
* Use reusable products.

For every suggestion display:

* Estimated carbon savings.
* Estimated money savings.
* Difficulty level.

## 7. Daily Eco Challenges

Examples:

* Walk for one day.
* Avoid plastic bottles.
* Eat one vegetarian meal.
* Turn off unnecessary lights.
* Recycle household waste.

Features:

* Mark as completed.
* Earn points.
* Maintain streaks.

## 8. Gamification System

Green Points:

* Completing challenges.
* Lowering carbon score.
* Daily logins.
* Maintaining streaks.

Badges:

* Eco Beginner
* Green Explorer
* Tree Saver
* Carbon Ninja
* Sustainability Hero

Display badge progress.

## 9. Leaderboard

Types:

* Global leaderboard.

* Weekly leaderboard.

Ranking based on:

## 10. Profile Page

Display:

* Profile picture.

* Achievements.
* Emission history.
* Settings.

## 11. Notifications System

Send reminders:

* Complete daily challenges.
* Update carbon data.
* Maintain streaks.
* Weekly progress reports.

## 12. Sustainability Learning Hub

Create an educational section containing:

* What is a carbon footprint?
