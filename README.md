# EcoQuest 

## About the Project

EcoQuest is a web application made to help people understand their carbon footprint and encourage them to adopt more eco-friendly habits. The idea behind this project is that many people want to live sustainably but they don't really know how much impact their daily activities have on the environment.

This app tries to solve that problem by calculating a user's estimated carbon emissions and giving them simple ways to improve and reduce it.

---

## Problem Statement

Design a solution that helps individuals understand, track and reduce their carbon footprint through simple actions and personalized insights.

---

## What the App Does

* Calculates estimated annual carbon footprint.
* Shows a dashboard with sustainability insights.
* Gives personalized recommendations.
* Provides daily eco challenges and missions.
* Rewards users with badges and points.
* Includes a leaderboard to make the experience more engaging.
* Has a learning section with information about sustainability.

---

## How it Works

The user enters some basic information like:

* Transportation method
* Daily travel distance
* Electricity usage
* Household size
* Number of flights taken per year
* Diet preference
* Shopping habits

Based on these details, the app estimates the user's carbon emissions and displays different statistics and suggestions.

The app also encourages users to complete eco-friendly tasks and maintain streaks to build better habits over time.

---

## Approach

While building this project, the main focus was on three things:

1. Make users aware of their environmental impact.
2. Help them track their progress.
3. Motivate them to reduce their emissions through small actions.

Instead of only showing numbers, the app uses gamification like badges, streaks and challenges to make the experience more interactive.

* The carbon values are estimated and may not be exactly accurate for every region.
* User inputs are assumed to be correct.
* The app is mainly created for awareness and educational purposes.
